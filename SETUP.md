## Design rationale

- **Selected concept — AI Systems Observatory:** presents a coherent engineering identity rather than a generic badge-and-stats profile.
- **Experience as a system:** the visual map explains inputs, orchestration, outputs, and impact in seconds.
- **Mastery as evidence:** the 74-node constellation creates curiosity, while the detailed vault preserves trust and scanability.
- **No flaky widgets:** the kit avoids third-party contribution cards and activity counters in the main narrative.
- **Theme-aware and accessible:** custom assets include light/dark variants, text alternatives, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
